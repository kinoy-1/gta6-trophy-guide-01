Platinum: Vice Got a Price! — earn the other 54.

      Built from the official descriptions: the story first, then the 100% checklist, then the cleanup. About 200 hours in all, one playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty setting — PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10.

      Rules of the road

      • No mission replay, no New Game+. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter — the game has 15 manual save slots.
      • Cheats switch trophies off. PowerPyx and GTABase both report that cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off the whole run.
      • Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. Everything for A Long Road can be done in free-roam after the story.
      • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. The only trophy that may be missable is Positive Mental Attitude — see the Missables tab.

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable, and even that one is unconfirmed. Everything else — story trophies included — can be earned in free-roam after the credits.

      Save discipline

      • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and one before the final mission.
      • In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning and follow her to the balcony to meditate.
      • Optional missions with black icons and pink letters disappear after a while — PowerPyx reports this does not matter for trophies, because there are far more than the 100 the checklist needs.
      • Quest givers who vanish for a while (Kenton, for example) come back later in the story, so do not panic.
